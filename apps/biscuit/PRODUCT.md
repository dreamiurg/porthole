# Biscuit

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
Kids who love dogs and reading, roughly 7 to 11, and strong readers. Content should reward inference and curiosity. This is a Tamagotchi-style game for them, beginning with a browser emulator for a Waveshare ESP32-S3 2.1-inch round touchscreen (480 × 480).

## Stack
Delegated by the user. Native HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Canvas, and localStorage; Node's built-in HTTP server and test runner. No runtime dependencies.

## Brand commitments
Retro pixel graphics, a cute dog companion, books, and creative play. Biscuit is affectionate, curious, and playfully doglike. Smart means rich ideas and satisfying discoveries; his everyday voice stays natural and warm. Avoid procedural, detective, or corporate language in companion dialogue.

## Scope and assumptions
The user delegated remaining decisions. English copy, a golden puppy named Biscuit, local browser saves, seven original branching mysteries, 96 sourced discoveries in twelve subjects, six animated tricks, daily adventures, twelve stickers, and three permanent growth stages. The final case has no physical buttons: every game control is on the touchscreen. No death, attendance streaks, or loss of earned progress for time away. This is a game/screen emulator, not CPU emulation or firmware. Exact board variant is not verified.
