# Radar

Live airplanes around the device's home, drawn as a radar scope: rings, N/E/S/W, each plane an arrowhead with a speed
line and a label. Launcher label `Radar`, NVS namespace `radar` (never renamed once shipped). RGB565, drawn in code with
its own font (B612 Mono Bold, the cockpit typeface; `fonts/OFL.txt`, generated by `tools/fontconv.py --game radar`). No
sounds. Ported from github.com/dreamiurg/ESP32-Plane-Radar (fork of MatixYo/ESP32-Plane-Radar): the idea and the
adsb.fi data, rewritten for this runtime.

On the board the feed is `net::Radar` (`firmware/net.cpp`: wifi, adsb.fi every 5 s, routes from adsbdb.com; see
`apps/porthole/AGENTS.md`); the sim and the tests run it against a canned feed.

- `os/air.h`: the feed contract (`air::Feed`, `Plane`, `Snapshot`, `State`), set with `air::setFeed` by the host
  before the shell starts; a game only reads it. Planes come as km east/north of home, never as coordinates. Home is
  one per device, set by a grown-up in the system menu (Info -> Home: a street address looked up once) and read by
  the feed (`System::home`); the game never sets it.
- `scope.*`: geometry with no drawing. `RANGE_MI` 3/6/12/25 (outer ring), `place` (inside the ring, or a rim dot at its
  bearing), `tagFor` (a label beside its plane, toward the middle, else away, above, below; the first spot wholly on the
  glass and clear of other labels, symbols, N/E/S/W, the menu and home buttons and the range label, trying last frame's
  side first so labels don't hop), words (`altText`, `rangeText`, `spotText`, `stateText`).
- `render.*`: the look (night navy, sea-green rings, amber/cyan/coral planes for airliner/small/military). Planes glide:
  each is drawn flown on from its last fetch by whole seconds (capped at `air::STALE_MS`), so the scope repaints once a
  second and on new data (`canvas::Frames` with a look hash), not every frame. Labels have a dark backing. Non-live
  states show a note above the middle. Home and the menu button are drawn smaller than their touch areas (24x22 box,
  14 px radius).
- `game.*`: two pages, scope and menu. A press counts on release when it started and ended on the target, however long
  it was held (small kids press and hold). Scope: home, the middle button (menu), anywhere else on the glass steps the
  range. Menu: OK, which way is up (N/E/S/W), range, label lines (Flight, Route, Alt), and home's coordinates, read
  only.
- `save.h`: per kid: range, up, hideCall/hideRoute/hideAlt (zero = shown). Version 1, 20 bytes, append-only from here.

Sim: `feed live|empty|nospot|nowifi|joining|lost|off` (see `host/sim.cpp`). Tests: `host/test_radar.cpp`. Playtests:
`tests/playtests/80_radar.txt`, `81_radar_monkey.txt`.
