# Rally's adaptive rivals: a win raises the kid's level on that track (by two when no rival is near: the fixture's
# wins are all that clear), a loss above 0 halves it, the level survives a reboot, belongs to one kid and one track,
# a never-raced track starts one below the kid's best (at most 3), and the level stays inside its range. Finishes come from the dbg
# fixtures (reachability), so this is a regression check, not evidence of how a race feels.
reset
tilt 0 1000 0
profile Zoe 9
app racing
wait 600
expect level=0
dbg lastlap
wait 5500
expect-screen race_results
expect place=1
expect level=2
# Replay: a second win
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastlap
wait 5500
expect place=1
expect level=4
# Replay: a loss above 0 halves the level
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect-screen race_results
expect place=4
expect level=2
# Replay: a win after the loss
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastlap
wait 5500
expect place=1
expect level=4
tap 80 140
wait 1500
reboot
wait 600
tap 80 67
wait 900
app racing
wait 600
