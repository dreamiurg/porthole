# Rally's adaptive rivals: a win raises the kid's level on that track (by two after a runaway: the fixture's wins
# lead all race), a loss from 1..3 drops it to 0 and from 4..9 to -2, the level survives a reboot, belongs to one kid
# and one track,
# a never-raced track starts one below the kid's best (inside 0..3), and the level stays inside its range. Finishes
# come from the dbg fixtures (reachability), so this is a regression check, not evidence of how a race feels.
