# The daily cap: 25 minutes of play a day per profile, across every game, even alone on the device. Used up, the
# rest screen says "Back tomorrow" with no countdown, the picker sends the kid back to it, and after local midnight
# the same kid gets in again. A minute without a touch is not play.
newgame Solo Rex
wait 2400
expect-screen home
expect today>0
expect today<10
dbg bedtime
wait 1500
expect-screen rest
expect today>=1500
expect rest>28000
ui-check rest-bedtime
snap c01_bedtime
# OK goes to the picker; picking the same kid shows the rest screen again
tap 80 132
wait 450
expect-screen picker
tap 80 67
wait 450
expect-screen rest
# 16:00 + 8 h: midnight, a new day, the rest screen gives way to the picker
skip 28800
wait 450
expect-screen picker
tap 80 67
wait 450
expect-screen launcher
expect rest=0
expect today=0
# play, then two minutes without a touch: only the first minute counts
tap 80 76
wait 2400
expect-screen home
wait 120000
expect today>55
expect today<70
sheet daily_cap
