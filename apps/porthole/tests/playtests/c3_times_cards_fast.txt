# Times Cards: a fast round, and a missed 1x fact drawn as dots. An 8-year-old knows 1x to 6x but still works out the
# facts with a 6, 8 or 9 in them slowly; once three of those are slow, the deck offers Fast! beside Go!: eight of
# them, slow ones first, with the round's time running at the top, and on the done page that time against the kid's
# best. Nothing else changes: the answers grade and schedule as in any round, a miss shows the right answer, and the
# round pays its stars. A 1 x n or n x 1 missed shows as one group of n dots or n groups of one, for 3.5 s.
profile Sam 8
app timescards
wait 600
dbg level7
wait 100
expect offers=0
ui-check cards_home
dbg slow
wait 600
expect offers=1
expect-screen cards_home
ui-check cards_home-fast
snap f01_home_fast
# Fast! deals the fast round: eight cards, the time running
tap 103 131
wait 600
expect-screen cards_ask
expect fastround=1
expect base=8
wait 2400
ui-check cards_ask-fast
snap f02_ask_timer
tap 80 70
wait 600
expect-screen cards_answer
snap f03_answer_timer
dbg wrong
wait 100
ui-check cards_answer-missed
snap f04_missed
wait 2400
expect-screen cards_ask
repeat 7
tap 80 70
wait 600
dbg right
wait 1100
end
expect-screen cards_done
expect wrong=1
expect stars>=2
expect newbest=1
ui-check cards_done-fast
snap f05_done_first_best
# Again: another fast round, answered slower: no new best, the best still shown
tap 80 133
wait 600
expect-screen cards_ask
expect fastround=1
repeat 8
tap 80 70
wait 1200
dbg right
wait 1100
end
expect-screen cards_done
expect newbest=0
ui-check cards_done-fast-slower
snap f06_done_best
# A missed 1 x 4: one group of four dots; 4 x 1: four groups of one
dbg card3
wait 600
tap 80 70
wait 600
dbg wrong
wait 300
expect-screen cards_answer
ui-check cards_answer-ones
snap f07_one_group
wait 2400
expect-screen cards_answer
wait 1200
expect-screen cards_ask
dbg card30
wait 600
tap 80 70
wait 600
dbg wrong
wait 300
snap f08_groups_of_one
sheet fast
