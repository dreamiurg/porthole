# Times Cards: multiplication flash cards with spaced repetition, one deck per kid. A 6-year-old starts on 2x and an
# 8-year-old on 4x. Go! (or a tap on the deck's card) deals a round: a first round is the table's first three facts at the
# starting pace, topped up to five with known 1x facts. Tapping the card shows the answer; Easy, Hard or Wrong grades it (a Wrong card comes back a few cards
# later). A fact counts as known after Easy on two separate days; at most six facts are learned at once, so the next
# table's facts start while the last of this one settle, and the level moves on once all ten are known. The house
# goes from a card back to the deck, and from the deck to the launcher. Every page is audited.
