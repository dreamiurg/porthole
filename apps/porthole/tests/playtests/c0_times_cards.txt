# Times Cards: multiplication flash cards with spaced repetition, one deck per kid. A 6-year-old starts on 2x and an
# 8-year-old on 4x. Go! (or a tap on the deck's card) deals a round card by card. Tapping the card turns it to four
# answers; the kid taps one. A right answer goes green, a wrong one red with the right one green, and the next card
# follows. The grade comes from right or wrong and the time taken against this kid's own usual time; fast right answers
# move the round's step up (new facts sooner, then a probe of the next table), a miss moves it down (easy wins). A
# missed card comes back a few cards later. A fact counts as known after right answers in two separate halves of a day
# and then on another day; the level moves on once a table is known. The house goes from a card back to the deck, and
# from the deck to the launcher. Every page is audited.
