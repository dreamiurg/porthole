# Times Cards: multiplication flash cards with spaced repetition, one deck per kid. A 6-year-old starts on 2x and an
# 8-year-old on 4x. Go! deals ten cards; tapping the card shows the answer; Easy, Hard or Wrong grades it (a Wrong card
# comes back a few cards later). A fact counts as known after Easy on two separate days, and once all ten facts of the
# table are known the next table opens. The house goes from a card back to the deck, and from the deck to the launcher.
# Every page is audited.
