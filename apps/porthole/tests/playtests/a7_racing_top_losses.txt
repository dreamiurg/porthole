# Rally's top levels (19-21 on Desert Canyon, where the Pro road hazards appear): a loss from the top drops the level
# by three per race down to 9, then to -2 like any loss from 4..9, so losing at the top takes several races to fall
# below 0; a win at the top stays at the top.
# Finishes come from the dbg fixtures (reachability), so this is a regression check, not evidence of how a race feels;
# the black-box pass is docs/racing/pro-playtest/README.md.
reset
tilt 0 1000 0
profile Zoe 10
app racing
wait 600
expect level=0
dbg lastlap
wait 5500
expect-screen race_results
dbg level 21
# a win at the top stays at the top
tap 52 111
wait 4000
expect race_level=21
dbg lastlap
wait 5500
expect place=1
expect level=21
# five losses in a row: 21 -> 18 -> 15 -> 12 -> 9 -> -2
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect-screen race_results
expect place=4
expect level=18
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect level=15
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect level=12
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect level=9
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect level=-2
