# Rally's top levels on Desert Canyon (top 12, Pro hazards at 10-12): a loss from the top steps down three a race
# (12 -> 9 -> 6 -> 3, then 0 like any loss from 1..3, then -2), so losing at the top takes several races to fall
# below 0; a win at the top stays at the top.
# Finishes come from the dbg fixtures (reachability), so this is a regression check, not evidence of how a race feels;
# the black-box pass is docs/racing/pro-playtest/README.md.
reset
tilt 0 1000 0
profile Zoe 10
app racing
wait 600
expect level=0
dbg lastlap
wait 5500
expect-screen race_results
dbg level 12
# a win at the top stays at the top
tap 52 111
wait 4000
expect race_level=12
dbg lastlap
wait 5500
expect place=1
expect level=12
# five losses in a row: 12 -> 9 -> 6 -> 3 -> 0 -> -2
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect-screen race_results
expect place=4
expect level=9
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect level=6
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect level=3
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect level=0
tap 52 111
wait 4000
dbg lastplace
wait 5500
expect level=-2
