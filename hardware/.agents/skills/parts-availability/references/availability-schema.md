# Availability report schema

## Recommended table

| Item / function | Exact spec and quantity | Channel class | SKU / seller | Region | Evidence date | Stock / lead time | MOQ / pack | Substitute fit | Confidence | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Confidence

- **High:** exact SKU, authoritative drawing, seller/region, and explicit current stock or lead time were checked.
- **Medium:** exact specification is verified but stock, shipping, or seller information is incomplete or volatile.
- **Low:** a search listing or approximate substitute is the only evidence; do not use it for a committed build date.

## Channel decision

State why the part belongs in its class. “Specialist” means a normal builder may need an industrial distributor, quote, or service bureau; it does not mean the part is custom. “Custom” means the geometry or process itself is not available as a verified catalog item.
